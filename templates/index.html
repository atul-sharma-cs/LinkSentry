LinkSentry

Analyze suspicious URLs and files to detect phishing, typosquatting, and other red flags — combining structural rule checks with AI semantic analysis.

We check the file name, extension, and hash for suspicious patterns — this is not a full antivirus scan of the file's contents.

By scanning, you agree this tool provides a best-effort risk signal, not a guarantee.

LinkSentry combines fast, offline structural checks (URL length, suspicious characters, known shortener domains, file extension patterns) with an AI model that reasons about brand impersonation and social-engineering language. Neither layer is a substitute for your own judgement or a dedicated antivirus product.
